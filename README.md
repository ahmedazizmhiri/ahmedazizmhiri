I value:

- **Business relevance before technical complexity**
- Clear and traceable decision logic
- Reliable data and explicit assumptions
- Measurable and understandable business outcomes
- Human review for uncertain or sensitive decisions
- Automation that improves accountability rather than hiding complexity
- Privacy and confidentiality in operational projects
- Honest documentation without unsupported claims
- Technology selected according to the problem, not the other way around
